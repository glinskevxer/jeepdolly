## ideas

| word | sense |
| --- | --- |
| bridge formula | The federal rule that says how much a run of axles may weigh given how far apart the first and last of them are. |
| crossover | The spread past which adding an axle stops raising what the bridge formula allows. Twelve times N times N minus one, in feet. |
| extreme | The distance from the first axle of a group to the last, measured centre to centre. |
| frost law | A seasonal order cutting the axle limits while the subgrade thaws, usually to sixty percent for about six weeks in spring. |
| headroom | How much more a carrier may be charged before the account hits its credit floor. |
| jeep dolly | An axle set dropped between the tractor and the trailer to spread gross weight across more axles. |
| nondivisible | A load that cannot be taken apart and hauled in two trips. Only a nondivisible load may be permitted over the statutory weight. |
| superload | A load big enough that the application goes to engineering review rather than being written over the counter. |
